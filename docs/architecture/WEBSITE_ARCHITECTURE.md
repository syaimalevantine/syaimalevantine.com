# Website Architecture

**Project:** Syaima Levantine

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** LOCKED

---

# Vision

Syaima Levantine is a knowledge hub that helps creators learn AI for pattern design, microstock, and productivity through practical experience, educational content, and high-quality digital products.

---

# Core Philosophy

Learn → Trust → Explore → Buy

The website prioritizes education before promotion and long-term trust over short-term conversions.

---

# Website Structure

```
Home
│
├── Products
│   ├── Pattern Studio AI
│   └── Microstock Intelligence Suite
│
├── Blog
│
├── About
│
└── Contact
```

---

# Homepage

Purpose:

- Introduce the brand
- Guide visitors
- Build trust
- Direct visitors to Products or Blog

Hero includes:

- Brand Logo
- Knowledge Hub tagline
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

---

# Products

Each product has its own landing page.

Current products:

- Pattern Studio AI
- Microstock Intelligence Suite

Future products should follow the same design system.

---

# Blog

The blog is the primary long-term traffic source.

Each article should:

- educate
- solve a problem
- link to related articles
- recommend relevant products when appropriate

---

# Content Categories

Primary topics:

- Pattern
- Microstock
- AI Productivity
- Business

---

# Information Architecture

Navigation:

- Products
- Blog
- About
- Contact
- Language Switcher

Maximum click depth:

3 clicks from Homepage.

---

# International Structure

Supported languages:

- English
- Indonesian
- Arabic
- Turkish

URL structure:

```
/en/
/id/
/ar/
/tr/
```

Each language has its own localized articles.

---

# Homepage Hero

The homepage hero is the visual identity of the brand.

Characteristics:

- Full screen
- Centered logo
- Olive photography background
- Minimal navigation
- Two call-to-action buttons
- Large negative space
